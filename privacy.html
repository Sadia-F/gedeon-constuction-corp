Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Gedeon Construction Corp (“Gedeon,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what information may be handled when you visit this website or contact us using the phone number or email shown here.

Information this website handles

This website does not have an account, payment, or inquiry form. If you call or email us, you choose what to share; that may include your name, contact details, project address or description, and information needed to respond. Phone calls and email are handled through your phone and email providers, which have their own privacy practices.

To deliver and protect the website, our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, referring page, and request time. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and renovation photographs from Unsplash. When your browser requests these assets, those providers may receive technical connection information. Their handling is governed by their own notices: GitHub Privacy Statement, Google Privacy Policy, and Unsplash Privacy Policy.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

As of the effective date above, we do not use this website to run analytics, behavioral advertising, or advertising pixels, and we do not set cookies for those purposes. Hosting or third-party asset providers may use technical measures or cookies under their own practices. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect some third-party features.

How we use information

Information you send us is used to respond to your inquiry, discuss a potential project, provide requested estimates, communicate about services, protect our systems, and meet legal obligations. We do not use this website to sell products or accept payments.

When information may be shared

We may use service providers to operate communications and the website, and information may be processed by the hosting and asset providers described above. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect people, rights, or security. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising through this website.

Retention and security

We keep messages and related information only as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a business relationship, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal requirements. Technical hosting logs are handled according to the hosting provider’s practices. We use reasonable care with information we receive, but no website, email, or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may choose not to contact us through the website. If you have a privacy question or want to ask about information you sent directly to us, email gedeonconstructioncorp1@gmail.com. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law. Rights and choices can depend on where you live and the circumstances.

Children and other websites

This website is for homeowners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information. This website links to services operated by other organizations; we do not control their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the website or our practices change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest version. Please check this page periodically.

This notice describes the website as currently configured. If the business adds forms, analytics, advertising, booking, or payment tools, this notice should be updated to reflect their actual data practices.
